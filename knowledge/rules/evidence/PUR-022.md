# Evidence — PUR-022 (pre-auth payment-option restriction)

- Rule: purchase `type: "pre-auth"` supports only ABA PAY, KHQR and Card
  payment — `cards`, `abapay_khqr`, `abapay_khqr_deeplink` (and legacy
  `abapay`/`abapay_deeplink` ABA-PAY forms). `alipay`, `wechat`,
  `google_pay` are NOT supported for pre-auth.
- Source: OFFICIAL_DOCUMENTATION
- URL: https://developer.payway.com.kh/purchase-14530820e0
- Retrieved: 2026-10-05 (first recorded in the DX platform audit pass 2,
  finding N-10, same date; re-verified live by the P0 slice evidence pass)

## Verbatim quotes

Purchase page, `type` field description:

> "Type of the transaction, default value is `purchase`. Supported value:
> `pre-auth`: for pre purchase, `purchase`: for full purchase. **Note:
> pre-auth only support ABA PAY, KHQR and Card Payment.**"

QR API page (`developer.payway.com.kh/qr-api-14530840e0`), stating the same
restriction from the excluded-methods side:

> "Alipay & WeChat do not support pre-auth."

## Derivation

Combined with the official purchase `payment_option` list (rule PUR-003:
cards, abapay_khqr, abapay_khqr_deeplink, alipay, wechat, google_pay), the
pre-auth-eligible subset is exactly {cards, abapay_khqr, abapay_khqr_deeplink}
— the three methods in the official "only support" sentence.

## Enforcement

`src/domains/checkout.ts#createCheckoutDomain` throws `PayWayConfigError`
citing PUR-022 when `type` resolves to `pre-auth` (explicit or default) and
`payment_option` is outside this set. The official text is unconditional, so
the rule is HARD (audit N-10 risk note: a profile-specific exception would
gain a `variants` entry, not weaken the rule).
