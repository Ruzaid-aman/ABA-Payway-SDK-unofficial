## Confirmed ALREADY-CORRECT (knowledge == code)

- Business-level 429-in-200 vs transport 429 classification (`PayWayRateLimitError` on both shapes).
- `return_url`/`callback_url` sent PLAIN (live-proven; the bot's base64 claim conflicts with the
  capture — kept, escalation filed).
- Token flags: `CITR_FIX` rejected on linking; subscription trio `ctid`+`CITR_FIX`+frequency.
- Expired-token local refusal before the gateway call (`cof charge --ctid`), `--token` bypass.
- `expired_at` from deliveries trusted as authoritative; renewal restarts the window.
- amount hashing (HMAC over the amount as sent; no lang/extra-field leakage — pinned).
- Two-domain lifetime split (generate-qr seconds min-180 / checkout minutes min-3 max-43200) —
  retained despite the bot's minutes claim; conflicts stay open.
- Payment-link pushback numeric `status: 0` accepted (string `"00"` tolerated).
- REFUND_ERROR_CODES already carried PTL37 (exceeds original).
